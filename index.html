LoungeThe idea behind Bodhirasa

The best journeys change the traveller, not just the view.

Bodhirasa joins two old ideas: awakening and essence. We travel to find the true character of a place, its people, food, craft and landscape. Somewhere along the way, something in you wakes up.

Go further

Past the viewpoints and souvenir streets, to boulder hills, backwater hamlets and temple lanes that see a few dozen visitors a year.

Stay closer

Homestays, not hotels. You share sadya, morning kolams and festivals with a host family who plan the days with us.

Leave better

Most of what you pay stays in the community, and every group joins a project the village has chosen.

10

travellers at most, so every group fits around one kitchen table.

60%

of each trip's cost paid directly to host families, guides and artisans.

3

nights minimum in each place. Long enough to learn names and be invited back.

We don't show you a place. We let a place show you who you are when you slow down.

      ArrivalsWhen you arrive

      From the arrivals hall to a seat at the family table.

      Every Bodhirasa trip follows the same arc. Here it is on our seven-day Hampi journey.

      1. 1
        Day 1

        Arrive

        Your host meets you at Hosapete station with an auto, a string of jasmine and hot filter coffee.

      2. 2
        Days 2–3

        Settle in

        Move into a family home across the Tungabhadra. Learn the morning kolam at the doorstep.

      3. 3
        Days 4–5

        Take part

        Cycle between ruins with a local guide, weave with a women's co-op and cook lunch to serve on banana leaves.

      4. 4
        Day 6

        Give back

        Join the village's chosen project: a school library, a water tank or a craft co-op stall.

      5. 5
        Day 7

        Head home

        A coracle ride at dusk, a keepsake made with your hosts, and a night bus home with new eyes.
